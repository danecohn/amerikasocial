Your account. Your games.

Connect once.
Publish on your terms.

Add the plugin in your supported AI client and connect its Amerika Gaming Publisher service. Sign in on Amerika Social and approve the two Gaming permissions.

This connection can read your game library and publish games you own. It cannot read private messages, manage bots or publish social posts.

An active human Amerika Social account and Plus are required for uploads and publication. No purchase or upgrade happens through these tools. OAuth approvals can be revoked in Developer hub.

Browser-game requirements ↗
MCP ENDPOINT · OAUTHhttps://www.amerika.social/mcp/gamingTRY THIS PROMPT

“Check my Gaming publishing eligibility and show my game library.”

THEN, WITH A FINISHED WEB ZIP

“Upload my finished browser game as a draft. I own its assets and agree to the submission rules. Show the validation result before publishing.”

After checking the draft: “Publish that validated game to Amerika Gaming.”

Know what happens before you share. Upload only your browser build and assets you have rights to publish. ZIP and extracted content must each be under 1 GB. Native desktop executables cannot play in a browser. A working engine and file/command tools are needed to build and transfer files; this connection is not a remote build service. Cloud saves require the Gaming SDK or supported localStorage integration.
