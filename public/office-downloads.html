Installation

Windows: Run the EXE installer. Choose ARM64 for Windows on ARM, or x64 for Intel/AMD PCs.

Mac: Open the DMG and drag Vigil Office to Applications. Choose Intel or Apple Silicon (M-series). macOS 12 or later.

Linux: Install the DEB with your package manager so its dependencies are installed too. Debian 12 / Ubuntu 22.04 or newer; choose x64 or ARM64 to match your system.

Early access packages. Windows builds are unsigned; Mac builds are not notarized by VigilTech. The desktop upstream is alpha, with English and Spanish spell checking included by default. Plain TXT/CSV opening is not included yet. Windows ARM64 uses the upstream x64 converter through Windows emulation.
