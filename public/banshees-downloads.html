01 / THE HOUSE

Get up. Check the door.

Walk through a detailed 3D security office, inspect dark halls with your flashlight, and operate the shutters in person. Recharging your light costs shared building power.

02 / THE ROUTINES

Four dogs. Familiar habits.

Banshee still wants ball time. Sheba waits by her kennel. Solely listens for the kitchen. Bella stays close. Learn what brings each of them home.

03 / THE VOICES

A real voice on the line.

33 newly recorded calls from Star, six playable 2D memories, an original BoxStudio soundtrack, and an unreliable building assistant with very bad advice.

ON YOUR MAC

Install, then clock in.

  1. Download and unzip the archive.
  2. Move Six Nights at Banshee’s.app into Applications.
  3. Open the app. Use WASD and mouse to move, E to interact, and F for your flashlight.

This build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, review the app in System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway only if you trust this download. No account or network connection is needed to play.

Made for Apple Silicon.
