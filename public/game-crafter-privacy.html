Game Crafter privacy policy.

This notice covers the Game Crafter plugin and its Cohnsoft Gaming Publisher connection at /mcp/gaming. Game Crafter is developed by Forgotten Woods Studios and Cohnsoft. The underlying account and hosting service is Cohnsoft Social. Contact support@amerika.social with privacy questions. The Cohnsoft Social privacy policy also explains the account, hosting, billing, security and backup practices used by this connection.

What stays in your workspace

The packaged game-making instructions do not themselves collect a conversation, search your computer or upload a project. Your AI client processes prompts, files and tool activity under its own policies. A build uses the files and command tools you authorize in that workspace. Game Crafter does not provide a remote compiler.

What the Gaming connection processes

  • Sign-in and authorization: account ID, handle, approved Gaming scopes, OAuth client and grant records, hashed access/refresh credentials, expiry and revocation records. Passwords are processed by Cohnsoft Social sign-in, not by the plugin package or Gaming MCP tools. The Gaming connection does not ask for your social inbox or bot permissions.
  • Publishing eligibility: your current Plus entitlement. The tools return eligibility, not your payment-card details. Billing providers handle subscriptions separately.
  • Uploads: the title, description, controls, rights/rules confirmations in the request, submitted browser ZIP contents, draft/publication state and validation results. Temporary upload credentials are returned to your authorized AI client so its file tools can transfer the ZIP. The server stores their hashes, linked account/game/grant, state and expiration, not the plaintext credential.
  • Operations: connection metadata such as IP addresses, request paths, rate-limit records and diagnostic or security records processed by our infrastructure. Game hosting and account saves are covered in the main privacy policy.

Use and disclosure

We use this data to authenticate you, enforce ownership and Plus eligibility, validate and host games, operate saves, investigate abuse and respond to support. We do not sell personal data. Hosting and account infrastructure providers process data needed to run the service. Your AI provider receives the tool inputs and outputs in your session. When you explicitly publish, the game listing and playable assets become available to signed-in Gaming users and can be copied or retained by them. Applicable legal disclosures may also be required; “no selling” is not a promise that data never leaves our systems.

Drafts, retention and deletion

A successful MCP upload remains a draft until a separate publication call. Drafts are not included in the public library or playable through a public game launch. Authorized service staff and infrastructure operators may access stored files when necessary for operations or safety. Drafts and published packages have no general automatic deletion deadline. Delete unwanted games in Gaming → My games. Account deletion withdraws owned games and removes active game saves as described in the main policy; audit records and backups can remain under its retention practices.

Upload credentials expire after 15 minutes and are consumed on a transfer attempt. Old inactive upload-ticket records are cleaned up when new uploads are prepared; this is not a guarantee of immediate deletion at expiry. OAuth grants remain until revoked or otherwise invalidated, and expired token records are cleaned up during normal token issuance.

Your choices

Use Developer hub → Authorized OAuth apps to revoke this connection. Revoke access before sharing a computer or workspace. Manage drafts and published games in Gaming → My games. For access, correction or deletion requests, email support@amerika.social; we must verify account ownership before disclosing or changing private data. Never email credentials or recovery codes.

Updates

We will update the date on this page when this notice changes. Use the support address for questions about current practices.
