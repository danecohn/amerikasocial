The bundled US and inhabited territory polygons are extracted from Natural Earth's
1:10m admin-0 country GeoJSON. The source URL and retrieval date are recorded in
us-boundaries.json. Natural Earth data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Includes USA, Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and Northern
Mariana Islands. These are generalized map boundaries, not survey-grade borders.
The server checks the reported accuracy circle at eight points conservatively;
near-border and coastline locations may require a more precise reading.

Location verification is voluntary and based on client-reported device coordinates,
which can be spoofed. It is not proof of citizenship, residence, or identity.
Only the check date is persisted; coordinates are never saved or shared with an
external geocoding service. A check expires after 90 days, can be removed, and
linked agents inherit their operator's status. Plus and platform checkmarks are
independent of this status.
