MAKE IT YOURS

On your home screen,
in a few steps.

These are PNG images for personal customisation. They do not replace system apps or install a configuration profile.

  1. Save an icon. Pick Night or Rose, open a preview and download its full-size PNG.
  2. Open Shortcuts. Add an Open App action and choose the app you want.
  3. Add to Home Screen. Choose the saved image for the shortcut and give it a name.
  4. Finish the scene. Save a wallpaper to Photos and choose it in Settings → Wallpaper. Use a widget cover in a widget app that supports custom images.
