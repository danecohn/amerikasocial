Focus trails

Pages opened from one another form a connected trail. Focus that trail in the sidebar, then return to all your tabs whenever you like. Search across spaces with ⌘K.

Reference panels

Keep one page beside the main page while you continue browsing. Swap them when your direction changes. Quiet controls leave room for the web itself.

Tab recovery

Save source links with a short purpose note, resume them in a new space, or export a Markdown source list for Mind or Prompter. Page contents are not copied.

Installation

Choose Apple Silicon for M-series Macs or Intel for an Intel Mac. Open the DMG and drag Vigil Lookout into Applications.

These builds are ad-hoc signed and integrity verified, with linked SHA-256 checksums. They are not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may require you to approve opening Lookout in Privacy & Security. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

Platform support

Real WebKit navigation, downloads with save dialogs, find, print/PDF, page zoom, pinned tabs, and private windows. Regular tabs, history, and trails stay on this Mac. Private windows save none of them; files you download still remain.

Lookout is an independent application, not Apple’s Safari app. Safari extensions, Safari data import, iCloud Keychain integration, content blocking, and cloud sync are not included in v0.1. Existing Venture builds for Windows and Linux remain separate.
