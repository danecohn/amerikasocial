A little help getting to play

Game Crafter support.

For help with Game Crafter, its publishing connection, game uploads, or privacy requests, email support@amerika.social.

Include your account handle, client name, Game Crafter version, what you expected, and the error message. For an upload, include the game ID and whether it is a Godot web export or another self-contained HTML5 game. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, OAuth tokens, temporary upload credentials, payment information or confidential game files unless support specifically arranges a safe transfer.

Connect the right service

Use https://vigiltech.org/mcp/gaming with OAuth and automatic client registration. The v1.0.2 package contains this connection. Sign in in the browser; never give your password to an AI or put it in connection settings.

Uploading a game through your AI

  1. Install Game Crafter v1.0.2 in a supported client that has file and command tools. Connect Vigil Gaming Publisher and approve access.
  2. Ask “Check my Gaming publishing eligibility and show my games.” Current Plus is needed to upload and publish.
  3. Export and test a self-contained browser game. Ask the AI to read the upload guide. Put index.html and its assets in a ZIP under 1 GB; no native EXE, APK, IPA or server code.
  4. Tell the AI which ZIP to upload, its title, description and controls. Confirm your publishing rights and agreement to the Gaming submission rules. It prepares a private draft and a one-use upload credential.
  5. The AI’s file/command tool sends the actual ZIP bytes with an HTTPS PUT. The credential is temporary and scoped to one game. If your client cannot transfer files, use the Gaming website upload instead; do not claim an upload happened.
  6. Ask the AI for the validation status. The game remains a draft. Say “Publish this game to Vigil Gaming” only when you want it publicly listed.

Common fixes

  • Plus required: sign in to the intended Plus account. This plugin cannot buy or grant a subscription.
  • Invalid or expired upload: prepare a fresh upload. Credentials expire after 15 minutes and cannot be reused; failed uploads also consume them. Remove unwanted drafts in Gaming → My games.
  • Game cannot run: export to Web, include every required file, and test the archive in a real browser. Single-threaded Godot WebGL 2 builds are supported; a native build cannot be converted by renaming it.
  • Connection revoked: reconnect using OAuth. You can revoke access in Developer hub → Authorized OAuth apps.

For general account help, see Vigil Social support. We do not promise a particular response time.
